**bithinker** builds products that help people grow their businesses. We start from how a business actually works and shape the software around it, so owners stop bending their company to fit a fixed tool.

## Products

| Product | What it does | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sora** | *Software for anything.* Describe how your business works in plain language and Sora builds the software for it: data, pages, dashboards and automations, live in your workspace. Keep changing it as the business changes. | In development |

## How we build

- **The business shapes the software**, not the other way round.
- **Nothing changes without you seeing it first**, and nothing is lost when it does.
- **Plain language over configuration**; configuration stays reachable for those who want it.
- **Built for everyone, everywhere**: no industry or region assumptions.

## The name

`bit` + `thinker`. The mark is two bits side by side: a filled square for **1** and an empty one for **0**. The same two bits dot the i's in the wordmark.
